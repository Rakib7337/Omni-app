# OmniDownloader v2.4.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** November 24, 2026  
**Platform:** Android 8.0 and newer

## Overview

OmniDownloader **v2.4.0** brings together one-tap video and playlist saving, ad-free video feeds, clean mobile browsing, and quick offline media editing inside a single Material Design 3 Android app—with no sign-up required.

---

## What’s New & Core Highlights

### 1. One-Tap Link & Platform Saver
- **Instant Link Paste:** Paste any supported URL or detect copied links automatically in Single or Batch mode.
- **Quick-Launch Platform Grid:** Direct shortcuts for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Bilibili, Dailymotion, X (Twitter), Reddit, Vimeo, and 1,000+ supported sites.
- **Direct Browser Switch:** Jump straight into the clean in-app browser from the Link Saver card.

### 2. Exact Video & Audio Format Picker
- **Video + Audio or Audio Only:** Switch output modes before downloading and preview clip duration, chapters, and captions.
- **Flexible Audio Formats:** Export directly to **MP3**, **M4A**, **OPUS**, or lossless **FLAC**.
- **Full Quality Control:** Save crystal-clear 4K Ultra HD, 1080p Full HD, or high-bitrate audio streams up to 320 kbps.

### 3. Ad-Free Video Feeds & Mini-Player
- **Clean Topic Tabs:** Browse **For You**, **All**, **Music**, **Gaming**, and **Tech** feeds with zero pre-roll ads or promoted feed clutter.
- **Uninterrupted Viewing:** Skips distracting sponsor breaks automatically and supports floating Picture-in-Picture and background playback.
- **Feed Search & Tuner:** Built-in feed search, manual refresh, and preference controls that stay private on your device.

### 4. Clean Mobile Browser & Offline Library
- **Pop-Up Protection:** Browse websites cleanly without redirects or banner clutter and save playable videos found on the page in one tap.
- **Organized Offline Library:** Track used media storage, detect duplicate files automatically, and filter saved items by **All**, **Videos**, **Audios**, or **Newest**.
- **Built-In Quick Actions:** Play, trim clips, extract audio, rename, or share files directly from every library card.
